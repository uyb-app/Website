Privacy Policy for the “Safehouse” app

Last updated: 16 November 2026 (added the optional online leaderboards and deletion without the app)

In short

Safehouse has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no user account with email or password. By default, all data stays on your device. We only receive data if you send us an email yourself or if you voluntarily join the online leaderboards (section 4).

2. Data on your device

The app stores data only locally on your device: game progress, best times and statistics, your settings and a log of your solving times (at most 3,000 entries, e.g. puzzle number, time, mistakes, hints used). This data never leaves your device. You can reset it in the app under Statistics; uninstalling the app deletes it.

The app uses the “vibrate” permission for haptic feedback. The “internet” permission is used only for the optional online leaderboards; the app then connects exclusively to our own leaderboard server.

3. Feedback by email

The feedback button in the settings opens your own email app with a prepared message. It contains the app version, device model, operating system version and your in-app settings (no solving times). You see the message before sending and decide yourself whether to send it.

If you write to us, we process your email address and the content of your message to answer your request and to improve the app (legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; legitimate interest in handling requests and improving the app). We delete the message once it is no longer needed for this purpose. We do not share it with third parties; it is delivered and stored by our email provider.

4. Online leaderboards (optional)

You can choose a player name in the app and – only if you explicitly switch it on – take part in online leaderboards. Participation is off by default. A name you choose without participating stays on your device.

If you take part, our leaderboard server processes:

  • your chosen player name and a randomly generated ID (not linked to your device, phone number or any account),
  • a random secret key per device that your app uses to identify itself and – only if you display it in the settings – a transfer code that lets you move your name and online times to another device (only a check value of each is stored),
  • your solving times per puzzle (first solve and best replay, each with whether hints were used) and the time of submission,
  • to prevent abuse when a name is created, a value derived from your IP address and obscured with a key that changes daily; it is deleted after 24 hours.

Your player name and times are shown publicly to all users of the app in the leaderboards. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time: in the settings you can stop taking part and use “Delete online data” to remove your name and all your entries from the server immediately. Without deletion, entries are kept as long as the leaderboards are operated.

The server runs on Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) as our processor (“Workers” and “D1” services). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses apply in addition. For every request, Cloudflare technically processes your IP address to establish the connection.

Please do not choose a name that identifies you directly (e.g. your full name). The server rejects offensive or misleading names.

Deleting your online data

You can delete your player name and all your entries in the online leaderboards at any time:

  • In the app: Settings → “Delete online data”. The data is removed from the server immediately and permanently.
  • Without the app (e.g. if you have already uninstalled it): send an email to support@uyb.app with your player name and – if available – your transfer code. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm this by email.

This deletes your player name, ID, device keys, transfer code and all solving times. Data on your device is not affected; it is removed when you uninstall the app.

5. App stores

If you download Safehouse from Google Play or the App Store, Google or Apple process data under their own responsibility; their privacy policies apply. From the stores we only receive aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. number of installs) and, where applicable, crash reports without names or contact details.

6. This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and protect it against abuse. See GitHub’s privacy statement for details.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Just write to support@uyb.app. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Children

Safehouse is suitable for all ages. The online leaderboards are optional; children under 16 should only use them with their parents’ consent.

9. Changes

If the app changes (for example with new features), we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
